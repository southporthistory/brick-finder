Standing on History

Hundreds of small pieces of Southport's story.

Southport's commemorative bricks mark the people, places and moments that make up the life of the community.

They celebrate birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, retirements and family homes; remember loved ones; and recognize organizations, businesses and people with ties to Southport.

Each brick also supports the Southport Historical Society's work to preserve and share the history of the community.
