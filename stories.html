The inscription is only the beginning

Every brick has a story.

A few engraved lines can tell us a name or a date, but not why the brick matters. If you know the person, family, organization, event or memory behind one of Southport's commemorative bricks, we'd like to hear from you.

Use the form to share what you know. The Southport Historical Society will review submitted information before using it as part of the public history of the brick program.
