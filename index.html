Southport's commemorative bricks

People. Places. Stories. Set in Stone.

Southport's commemorative bricks tell hundreds of small pieces of our community's story. They honor people, families, friendships, organizations, businesses, milestones and memories connected to Southport.

Some celebrate birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, retirements or a family's Southport home. Others remember loved ones or recognize organizations and people who have been part of the life of the community.

The Southport Historical Society's Commemorative Brick Program gives people with a connection to Southport a permanent way to mark someone or something meaningful to them while supporting the Society's work to preserve and share Southport history.

Purchasing a Brick

Brick orders are accepted each year from October 1 through November 30. New bricks are installed the following spring, generally by Memorial Day.

4" × 8" Brick — $100
Recommended: up to 3 lines with 18 characters per line.
Maximum: 4 lines with 21 characters per line.
8" × 8" Paver — $150
Recommended: up to 6 lines with 18 characters per line.
Maximum: 8 lines with 21 characters per line.

Spaces and punctuation count as characters. Engraving is limited to standard keyboard characters; special symbols cannot be used.
