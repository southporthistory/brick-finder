SHS Brick Finder — GitHub-ready Front Walkway photo update

Contents:
- bricks.json: corrected dataset with 714 currently indexed Fort Johnston bricks
  - 536 Front Walkway
  - 178 Side Walkway
- assets/bricks/: 536 web-sized Front Walkway photographs

Upload/replace these paths in the brick-finder repository:
1. Replace the repository's bricks.json with this bricks.json.
2. Upload the contents of assets/bricks/ into assets/bricks/.
3. Commit the changes.
4. After GitHub Pages deploys, test several Front Walkway search results and confirm their photographs display.

Old Jail bricks are not included in this dataset.
Side Walkway records remain in bricks.json but this package does not add Side Walkway photographs.
